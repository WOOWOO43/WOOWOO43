## 🏈 Football Analytics Work

*Built for coaching staff; code and data are kept internal.*

**Decision models & game-day tools**
- **Fourth-down decision model** — conference-wide "should they go vs. will they go" model built on play-by-play data, with heat maps of each team's go-for-it tendencies under its current head coach
- **Live booth tendency tool** — predicts run vs. pass and play direction (left / middle / right) from in-game personnel groupings
- **4th-down & situational chart guides** — weekly game-book remakes with coach-friendly guide pages for 4th down, two-point, and end-of-half decisions
- **Win the Situations scorecard** — per-game tracker of 10 situational battles (red zone, third down, two-minute, etc.)
- **Conference analytics** — home-field advantage, penalty, and fourth-down compliance analysis across a full conference

**Player evaluation & roster tools**
- **Group of 5 / Pac-12 / FCS player proficiency apps** — Shiny leaderboards with position-group grades, percentile color coding, and depth-chart role-slot comparisons
- **Snap-weighted roster valuation** — allocates a roster budget across position rooms based on snaps and grades
- **Digital depth / roster board** — shared drag-and-drop nameplate board for coaches
- **Snap count reports & game-count tracker** — weekly participation reports and season game tracking for redshirt management
- **Central analytics hub** — consolidating game-day, player-evaluation, and recruiting apps into one password-protected Shiny portal

**Data infrastructure**
- **Multi-division scrapers** — player bios and game-by-game stats for FBS, FCS, D2, and JUCO teams via ESPN; depth charts for every Group of 5 program
- **Weekly data pipelines** — merging weekly PFF exports into season-to-date datasets
- **Sports science pipelines** — Catapult GPS and VALD ForceDecks data pulled through their APIs into SQLite for analysis

**Operations**
- Opponent scouting packets, travel packets, pre-game timelines, and away-stadium warm-up maps for each road trip
